Key chart

Click any key to add it to your text. Consonants take the vowel you type after them: k + a gives কা.

Good to know
  • The letter o is the built-in vowel: kol gives কল.
  • Two consonants in a row join into a conjunct: kSha gives ক্ষা, stri gives স্ত্ৰি.
  • Capital letters matter: T Th D Dh N R S O U I H are different sounds from their small versions.
  • Put _ between letters to stop them combining: n_g gives নগ, not ং.
  • Wrap English words in curly braces to keep them as they are: {Google} khuji.
  • x also works for স, and ch for চ.
